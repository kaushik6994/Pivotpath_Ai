# Cedar training record procedure

Version: 1.0

## Purpose
Describe how the fictional Cedar training team records a completed practice session. This document is software demonstration material and is not an operating procedure for real pharmaceutical work.

## Scope
Applies only to the Atlas demonstration workspace and the fictional Cedar training exercise. It does not authorize access to production systems, materials, or equipment.

## Responsibilities
The participant completes the practice exercise and records the outcome. The training coordinator checks whether the record is readable and whether the required fields have been completed. A different reviewer checks the completed record before acceptance.

## Procedure
Enter the participant name, course title, completion date, and reviewer name in the Cedar training register. Include the course version so another person can identify the material used. If an entry is missing, return the record to the participant for correction. Keep the previous record available when a correction creates a new version.

This draft intentionally omits its accountable owner, effective date, and reference section so the Atlas rulebook can find them.
